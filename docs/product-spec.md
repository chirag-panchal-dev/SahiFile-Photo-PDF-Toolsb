# SahiFile: Photo & PDF Tools — Product Specification

## 1. Product Summary
- **App Name**: SahiFile
- **Subtitle / Tagline**: Photo & PDF Tools
- **Slogan**: Make every file upload-ready.
- **Google Play Title**: SahiFile: Photo & PDF Tools
- **Target Audience**: Students, job applicants, exam takers, and individuals submitting government forms who need photos, signatures, and PDFs adhering to strict upload limits.

## 2. Core Capabilities
1. **Photo & Signature Resizing**: Exact pixel dimensions with lockable aspect ratios and rotate tools.
2. **Target File Size Compression**: Bounded search encoding on JPEG quality to ensure output files are strictly under user-specified KB limits without guessing.
3. **Alpha / Transparency Preservation**: Flattening transparent PNG signatures onto selectable background colors (white, off-white, light blue) so backgrounds never turn black unexpectedly when encoded to JPG.
4. **Images to PDF**: Convert multiple gallery photos into standard A4, Letter, or Fit-to-image PDFs with configurable margins and orientations.
5. **PDF Merge**: Safe on-device combination of 2 or more PDF documents preserving document order.
6. **PDF Optimization**: On-device structural compression and stream cleanup with truthful before/after byte measurements.
7. **Preset Templates**: Bundled templates for Passport Photos, Signatures, SSC/UPSC Exams, PAN Card, and user-created custom presets.
8. **Local History**: Recent file tracking with search, category filtering, sharing, and private file deletion.

## 3. Privacy & Offline Guarantee
- 100% of image and document processing runs on the physical device.
- Zero network transmission of documents or filenames.
- No logins or account credentials required.
